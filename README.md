# Northstar Office Supplies LLC
## 2025 Year-End Business & Financial Performance Analysis

### Project Overview

This project presents a year-end financial and operational performance analysis for Northstar Office Supplies LLC, a fictional B2B office supplies distributor.

The objective is to evaluate the company's 2025 business performance, identify financial and operational risks, and develop actionable recommendations to support better decision-making and financial planning for 2026.

**Note:** All company information and transaction data are fictional and generated for portfolio and analytical practice purposes.

### Business Objectives

- Analyze annual and monthly revenue performance.
- Evaluate expenses and cost structure.
- Assess profitability and financial margins.
- Review cash flow performance.
- Evaluate operational KPIs.
- Identify business risks and improvement opportunities.
- Develop recommendations and a 2026 financial roadmap.

### Key Business Insights

**1. Revenue Performance**

- Annual net revenue reached $776,107.64.
- Revenue increased by approximately 36% from January to December.
- December was the strongest revenue month, while February was the weakest.

**2. Cost Structure**

- Cost of Goods Sold represented approximately 43.9% of net revenue.
- Payroll represented approximately 20.0%.
- COGS and payroll were the two largest expense categories.

**3. Profitability**

- The business generated a 56.1% gross margin.
- Operating margin reached 16.1%.
- Estimated net margin was 12.5%.

**4. Cash Flow**

- Ending cash increased from $42,000 to $92,372.22.
- Three months recorded negative net cash flow.
- The cash flow model requires reconciliation with transaction payment statuses.

**5. Operational Performance**

- Average on-time delivery was approximately 88.9%.
- Average return rate was approximately 4.2%.
- Delivery performance weakened during the second half of the year.

### Recommendations

1. Improve cash collection monitoring and reconcile payment records.
2. Strengthen delivery performance through carrier and regional analysis.
3. Investigate product returns and their underlying causes.
4. Introduce rolling cash flow forecasting.
5. Review supplier costs, freight expenses, and product profitability.
6. Improve revenue mix through profitable products and repeat customers.

### 2026 Financial Roadmap

The proposed roadmap focuses on four priorities:

- Q1: Financial control and cash flow management.
- Q2: Delivery reliability and return reduction.
- Q3: Profitability and revenue mix improvement.
- Q4: Financial performance review and 2027 planning.

### Data Limitations

This project uses synthetic practice data and simplified financial assumptions. Estimated tax is calculated at 22% of positive monthly operating profit.

The payment-status labels in the sales and expense ledgers do not reconcile with the simplified cash flow model. Therefore, outstanding receivables and pending payables have not been treated as verified financial balances.

The analysis is not an audited financial statement.
